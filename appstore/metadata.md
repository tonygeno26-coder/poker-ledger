## Description

**Limit: 4000 characters** · current length: ~1967

```
Pocketbooks Poker is the cash-game ledger and session tracker built for private poker hosts who want books that stay accurate from the first buy-in to final settle-up.

Run every private cash game with one clear house book. Start a session with location, date, stakes, and game type. Add players and staff as they sit. Log buy-ins as cash, marker, or e-cash so the table and the ledger stay in sync.

Markers & signatures
Issue markers when a player sits without cash. Capture a signature on the marker pad, then track balances live as the night unfolds. When they stand, record chip turn-in and see who owes the house—or who the house owes—update instantly.

Chip float & cash accounting
Track chips on hand, cash on hand, and e-cash separately with host-grade formulas: chip float, buy-ins dealt, cash-outs returned, dealer banks, starting bank, payouts, staff payouts, and expenses. Tips and drop stay in the profit picture without distorting your chip inventory.

Tips, drop & staff
Log tip-outs, house drop, and staff tax withheld alongside player action so dealer and floor numbers live in the same book as the table. Bonus chips and expenses roll into estimated profit so you know how the night performed before anyone leaves.

Settle-up & history
When the game ends, settle-up shows who owes, who’s owed, and what still needs to clear. Keep past sessions on device with summary cards, expandable player and staff lists, and a plain-text export you can paste into Messages, Notes, or email.

Private by design
Pocketbooks Poker is accounting software for private social games—not a casino app, not online gambling, and not a place to place bets. Your session ledger stays on your device. Optional account and subscription unlock cloud-ready features when you need them; core bookkeeping is built for the host at the table.

Made for home games and private tables. Just a clean ledger for the host.

Desert Spore / Pocketbooks Poker
https://pocketbookspoker.com
```
